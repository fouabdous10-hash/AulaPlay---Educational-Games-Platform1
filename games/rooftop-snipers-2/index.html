About Rooftop Snipers 2

Rooftop Snipers 2 is a fast two-player shooting game where stickman characters battle on rooftops and try to knock each other off the platform. The goal is to score points by landing shots that send your opponent away from the stage. Both players jump, move, and fire while dealing with loose ragdoll-style movement. The controls are simple, but controlling your character while aiming can take some practice. Each round is short, making it easy to play several matches in one session.

The battles happen on rooftops with platforms and different obstacles that can change how each round plays out. Your character jumps automatically or moves in a bouncing style, while you control the direction and shooting. You need to watch where your opponent is moving and choose the right time to fire. A direct hit can push them toward the edge, but missing a shot can leave you in a risky position. Depending on the stage, objects or moving platforms may also affect your movement and create new ways to avoid attacks.

Rooftop Snipers 2 is a good choice for local multiplayer fun with a friend. You can challenge each other in quick rounds and see who can score the most points. The unpredictable movement can create funny moments when players jump over each other or fall after a badly timed attack. You do not need complicated combos or long tutorials to get started. If you enjoy simple stickman battles with shooting, jumping, and physics-based movement, this sequel offers another set of rooftop fights to play in your browser.

How to Play

Use the movement and shooting controls assigned to your character. Jump around the rooftop and aim your weapon toward the other player. Fire when you have a clear chance to push the opponent toward the edge. Try to time your jumps so you can avoid incoming shots while keeping your character on the platform.

Keep moving instead of staying in one place for too long. Watch your opponent's jumps and wait for a useful opening before firing. You can also use the stage layout to block attacks or change your position. In two-player mode, each person uses their own controls, so make sure both players know their keys before starting. Knock your opponent off the rooftop to win the round and continue playing for a higher score.
