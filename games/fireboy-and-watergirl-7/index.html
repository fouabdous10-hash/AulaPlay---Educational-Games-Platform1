About Fireboy and Watergirl 7

Fireboy and Watergirl 7 is a cooperative puzzle-platform game where two characters must work together to reach the exits of each level. Fireboy can safely move through fire but must stay away from water, while Watergirl can travel through water but cannot touch fire. You control both characters and use their different abilities to solve puzzles. The main goal is to guide them through the temple, collect gems when possible, and reach their matching doors safely.

Each level is built with platforms, switches, moving objects, doors, hazards, and different paths. Some areas can only be reached by Fireboy, while others are made for Watergirl. This means you often need to move one character first and then use their position to help the other continue. Buttons may open doors or activate platforms, while levers and other objects can change the path through a room. The levels become more challenging as new puzzle setups require careful movement and cooperation between both characters.

Fireboy and Watergirl 7 can be played alone by controlling both characters or with another player on the same device. The controls are simple, but solving a level requires you to think about both characters at the same time. You need to avoid hazards, choose the right route, and make sure neither character gets left behind. If you enjoy teamwork puzzles and platform games, this adventure offers many rooms to explore and plenty of small problems to solve.

How to Play

Control Fireboy and Watergirl separately using their assigned movement keys. Move them across platforms and keep each character away from the element that can harm them. Fireboy should avoid water, while Watergirl should avoid fire. Green hazards are dangerous to both characters.

Use buttons, switches, doors, elevators, and moving platforms to create a safe route. Sometimes one character needs to stand on a switch while the other passes through an opened door. Look at the whole room before moving so you can decide which character should go first.

Try to collect the red gems with Fireboy and the blue gems with Watergirl when they are safely reachable. Then guide both characters to their matching exits. If one character gets stuck or touches a hazard, restart the level and try a different order of moves. Careful teamwork is the key to getting through the temple.
