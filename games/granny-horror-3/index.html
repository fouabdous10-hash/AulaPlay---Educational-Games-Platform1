About Granny Horror 3

Granny Horror 3 is a first-person survival horror game where escaping a haunted mansion is not as simple as finding the front door. Three dangerous enemies are waiting inside the house, and each one has a different way of hunting you. Granny reacts to sounds, Grandpa carries a shotgun and can attack when he spots you, while Slendrina appears in dark areas and creates another threat to watch out for. You have five days to explore the mansion, collect useful objects, solve puzzles, and discover a working escape route. Every room could contain something important, but searching carelessly can bring an enemy straight to your hiding place.

The mansion has locked doors, hidden compartments, security systems, traps, and several objects that you need to collect before you can make progress. You will need to remember where you found certain items and think about how they might work with other objects around the house. Beds and wardrobes can help you hide when an enemy gets close, but staying in one place for too long will not get you out. The game also includes different difficulty settings, so you can begin with Practice mode to learn the mansion or choose a much tougher challenge. If you enjoy stealth games, creepy locations, and escape puzzles, Granny Horror 3 gives you plenty of reasons to check every corner carefully.

Three Enemies, Three Different Threats

The biggest challenge in Granny Horror 3 is dealing with three enemies who do not all behave in the same way. You cannot use one strategy against everyone. Paying attention to their movements and learning what attracts them is an important part of surviving.

  • Granny: Granny listens for sounds around the mansion. Dropping objects, making noise, or walking across creaking floorboards can reveal your location. Move with care, avoid unnecessary noise, and think before interacting with objects in rooms where she might be nearby.
  • Grandpa: Grandpa carries a shotgun and becomes a serious threat when he spots you. Avoid standing in open areas where he has a clear view. Use doors, corners, furniture, and hidden routes to break his line of sight before moving to another room.
  • Slendrina: Slendrina can appear unexpectedly in dark corridors. If you see her, look away immediately. Staring at her can distort your vision and make it difficult to move safely through the mansion.

These enemies make every trip through the house a risk. A room that was safe a moment ago might become dangerous when someone hears you or enters from another direction. Learn which hiding places are available and avoid rushing through unfamiliar areas.

Search the Mansion for Useful Items

Exploration is a major part of Granny Horror 3. The mansion contains drawers, cabinets, hidden compartments, and other places where important objects can be found. Some items open locked doors, while others help you repair systems or reach areas that were previously inaccessible.

Keep an eye out for keys, spark plugs, weapons, and structural parts. You may find an item without immediately knowing where it belongs. Instead of ignoring it, remember its location and check the rooms you have already explored. A small object can be the missing piece needed to complete a larger puzzle.

The game also includes multi-step challenges involving locked gates, security barriers, power systems, and escape mechanisms. You may need to collect several objects before one of these systems can be used. Take a moment to understand what each area requires instead of trying random interactions. The Teddy bear is another special object that can trigger unique interactions and hidden objectives, so it is worth paying attention to when you discover it.

Use Hiding Places and Avoid Traps

Running away is not always the best option. When an enemy is close, a bed or wardrobe can give you a chance to hide until the danger passes. You should also pay attention to the layout of each room. Furniture, doorways, and corners can help you avoid being seen, especially when Grandpa is nearby.

Bear traps are another problem to watch for while exploring. Getting caught can leave you in a dangerous position, particularly if an enemy is already searching the area. You can escape a bear trap by looking down and using the F key. Crouching is also useful when you need to move carefully or hide from nearby threats.

Try not to enter every room without checking what is around you. Listen for movement, look for possible hiding places, and avoid making noise when you do not need to. A little patience can save one of your five available days.

Five Difficulty Settings

Granny Horror 3 offers five difficulty options, giving you control over how challenging the escape attempt will be. If you have never played this type of horror game before, Practice mode is a useful place to begin. It allows you to explore without active enemies, giving you time to learn the mansion and understand how its puzzles work.

  • Practice: Explore the mansion without active enemies and learn where important objects and rooms are located.
  • Easy: Enemies move at a reduced speed, and creaking floorboards are removed, making exploration more manageable.
  • Normal: A standard challenge that requires careful movement, searching, and planning.
  • Hard: Enemies create more pressure, so mistakes can become difficult to recover from.
  • Extreme: The most demanding setting for players who want a serious survival challenge.

There are also custom options that let you change which enemies appear. You can select Granny, Grandpa, or both, and choose whether Slendrina should be active. Additional options include Limping and Nightmare effects, along with the ability to skip the introduction. These settings give you different ways to experience the same mansion and create new challenges on later attempts.

Controls for Granny Horror 3

The game uses keyboard and mouse controls for movement, looking around, and interacting with objects. Take a little time to learn the controls before entering a difficult area. Knowing how to drop an item or crouch quickly can be useful when an enemy is nearby.

  • W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys: Move around the mansion.
  • Mouse: Look around and check your surroundings.
  • E or Left Click: Interact with objects and open doors.
  • G: Drop an item.
  • C or Left Ctrl: Crouch or hide.
  • F: Escape a bear trap while looking down.
  • Esc or Tab: Pause the game or open settings.

Tips for Escaping the Mansion

Your five-day limit means that wasting time can make an escape attempt much harder. You do not need to collect every object you see, but you should pay attention to items that might be connected to locked areas or escape systems. These simple tips can help you make progress:

  • Learn the layout first: Remember the location of important rooms, hiding places, and locked doors.
  • Keep track of items: If you find a key or mechanical part, remember where you picked it up and look for places where it might be useful.
  • Stay quiet around Granny: Avoid dropping objects or making unnecessary noise when she could be nearby.
  • Watch Grandpa's position: Do not cross open rooms when he has a clear view of your location.
  • Look away from Slendrina: Do not keep staring at her when she appears in a corridor.
  • Use hiding places wisely: Beds and wardrobes can help you avoid enemies, but plan your next move before leaving cover.
  • Try Practice mode: If you are stuck, explore without active enemies and learn how the mansion works.
