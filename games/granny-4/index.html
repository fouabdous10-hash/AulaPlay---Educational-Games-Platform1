About Granny 4

Granny 4 is a horror escape game where you must survive inside a creepy house while searching for a way out. The main goal is simple: explore the rooms, find useful items, solve puzzles, and escape before Granny catches you. The house has many locked doors, hidden areas, and objects that can help you move forward. You need to pay attention because even a small mistake can make noise and reveal your location.

The game gives you a large house to explore, with different rooms and paths to check. Keys, tools, switches, and other objects are placed around the map. Some items may be hidden in drawers or behind locked areas, so searching is an important part of each attempt. You may also need to complete several steps before reaching the final exit. The layout can make you change your route when Granny is nearby.

Granny 4 is mainly about exploration, puzzle solving, and staying quiet. Each attempt gives you a chance to learn the house and remember where important items can appear. If you get caught, you can try again and use what you learned. The game is a good choice if you enjoy browser horror games with simple controls and tense escape challenges.

How to Play

Start by exploring the house carefully and look for anything that may help with your escape. Pick up useful items and check locked doors to learn what you still need. Avoid making unnecessary noise when moving around the house. If Granny starts coming toward you, find a safe place and wait until the area is clear. Try to remember useful locations so you can plan your next route.

Some puzzles require you to collect items in a certain order or use one object to reach another area. Keep searching even when a room looks empty, as important clues can be easy to miss. If you enjoy this style of game, you can also try Granny 2, Granny 3 Return The School, Granny Creepy House, and Haunted School 2.
