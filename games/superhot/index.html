About Superhot

Superhot is a first-person action game built around one unusual rule: time moves only when you move. Your goal is to survive each level, defeat the red enemies, and reach the end of the stage. When you stand still, the world almost stops, giving you time to think about your next move. When you walk, aim, or attack, time starts moving again. This makes every action important and turns simple fights into careful decisions.

The game places you in small areas filled with red enemies, weapons, and objects that you can use during a fight. Enemies may approach with different weapons, so you need to watch their movements and decide what to do next. You can pick up weapons, throw objects, dodge attacks, and move around the level while controlling the pace of the action. Each stage has a clear objective, usually requiring you to deal with every enemy before you can continue.

Superhot is different from a normal shooting game because rushing is not always the answer. You have time to look around and plan a route before making your next move. One stage may give you a pistol, while another may place you near several objects or enemies. As you progress, the situations become more challenging and require careful planning. If you make a mistake, you can restart the stage and try a different approach. The simple visual style also makes enemies and important objects easy to spot.

How to Play

Use the movement and aiming controls to move through each stage. Remember that time advances when you move, so think before taking your next step. Look around for weapons and objects that can help you deal with nearby enemies. Aim carefully and avoid moving into a dangerous position without a reason.

Try to plan several actions ahead when there are multiple enemies around you. You can pause your movement to study their positions and then decide which target to handle first. Use the environment when it gives you a useful option, and restart the level if your plan does not work. Learning from each attempt will help you complete stages with more control.
