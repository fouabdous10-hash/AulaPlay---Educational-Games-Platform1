About Space Waves

Space Waves is a fast arcade game where you control a small spaceship and guide it through a dangerous space course. Your goal is to reach the end of each level without crashing into walls, spikes, or other obstacles. The ship moves forward automatically, so your main task is to control its height and direction at the right time. The levels are short, but they can require quick reactions when the path becomes narrow or changes direction.

The game takes place across a series of colorful levels filled with sharp corners, narrow passages, blocks, and other hazards. You need to move the ship up and down to stay inside the safe path. Some sections are open and give you time to prepare, while others require several quick movements in a row. Each level has its own layout and obstacle pattern, so learning the course can help you make progress. Reaching the finish line completes the level and lets you move on to the next challenge.

Space Waves focuses on timing, control, and repeated attempts. There is no large character system or complicated equipment to manage. You simply choose a level, control your ship, and try to reach the end. If you crash, you can restart and attempt the same section again. This makes the game easy to pick up but gives you a reason to keep practicing. If you enjoy games where one small mistake can restart a level, Space Waves offers plenty of short challenges to work through.

How to Play

Use the control button to move your spaceship upward and release it to let the ship move downward. Keep the ship inside the open path and avoid touching the walls or obstacles. Watch the space ahead and start adjusting your position before reaching a narrow section.

Try to keep your movements small when the path becomes tight. Holding the control for too long can send the ship too high, while waiting too long can make it drop into an obstacle. If you crash, remember the section that caused the problem and change your timing on the next attempt. With practice, you can learn each level and reach the finish without hitting the hazards.
