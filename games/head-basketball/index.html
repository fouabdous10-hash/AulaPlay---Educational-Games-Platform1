About Head Basketball

Head Basketball is a fun one-on-one basketball game where you control a cartoon-style player and try to score more points than your opponent. The goal is simple: shoot the ball into the basket while defending your own hoop. Matches are played on a small court, so there is not much space to waste. You need to move, jump, block shots, and time your attacks if you want to take control of the game.

The game gives you different characters to choose from, with each player having their own look and special abilities. During a match, you can move across the court, jump for the ball, shoot from different positions, and use special moves when the chance is right. You can also defend by jumping in front of shots or trying to take the ball away. Matches are usually short, which makes it easy to play a few rounds when you have some free time.

Head Basketball is mainly about timing, movement, shooting, and quick decisions. A good lead can disappear quickly if you stop defending, so you need to pay attention on both sides of the court. Learning how each character plays can also help you choose one that matches your style. If you enjoy simple sports games with cartoon graphics, short matches, and plenty of action, Head Basketball is a fun browser game to try.

How to Play

Use the movement controls to run around the court and position your player near the ball. Jump to reach high shots, block an opponent, or grab the ball. When you have possession, aim your shot toward the basket and choose a good moment to throw. You can also use special moves when they are available.

Keep defending your basket when your opponent has the ball. Try to learn their movement and react when they prepare to shoot. Do not focus only on scoring because stopping easy baskets can make a big difference. Practice your jumps, shots, and special moves to improve your results and win more matches.
