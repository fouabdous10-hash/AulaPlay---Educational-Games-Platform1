About Obby: Supercar Race on Keyboard

Obby: Supercar Race on Keyboard is a motorcycle driving game where you ride through busy roads and try to travel as far as possible without crashing. Your main goal is to control the bike, avoid cars, and complete different driving challenges. Traffic moves around you from several directions, so you need to watch the road and choose the right moment to pass other vehicles. The game focuses on quick decisions, careful steering, and keeping your bike under control.

As you ride, you may find several road situations that test your driving skills. Some sections have heavy traffic, while others give you more open space to build speed. You can ride between cars, pass vehicles closely, and collect useful items when they appear on the road. Different missions or game objectives can give each run a clear target. Depending on the mode, you may need to reach a certain distance, complete a task, or earn enough points before the run ends.

Obby: Supercar Race on Keyboard keeps its controls simple, which makes it easy to start playing in a browser. The main challenge comes from judging traffic and reacting before a car gets too close. You also need to avoid sudden movements that can send your bike into another vehicle. If you enjoy motorcycle games with busy roads, short challenges, and score-based gameplay, Obby: Supercar Race on Keyboard is a good choice for a quick gaming session. Each attempt gives you a chance to improve your distance and handle traffic with more confidence.

How to Play

Use the movement controls to steer your motorcycle along the road. Watch the vehicles ahead and change your position when you see a safe opening. Try to keep enough space around your bike so you have time to react when traffic changes.

Passing cars can help you earn points, but do not move into a gap unless you have enough room. Pay attention to vehicles coming from nearby lanes and avoid sudden turns. On open sections of the road, you can maintain a steady speed while preparing for the next group of cars.

Complete the current objective before the run ends. If you crash, think about what caused the mistake and try again with a different approach. Practice will help you learn how traffic moves and when to change lanes. Focus on the road ahead, keep your bike under control, and aim for a longer run each time.
