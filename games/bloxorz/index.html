About Bloxorz

Bloxorz is a classic block puzzle game where your main job is to guide a rectangular block across floating platforms and place it into a square hole. The idea sounds simple, but each level asks you to think carefully before making a move. You control the block one step at a time, rolling it across tiles and trying not to fall off the edges. The goal is to reach the marked hole and stand the block upright in the correct position.

The game has many puzzle levels, and each one introduces a different layout to solve. Some stages have narrow paths, small platforms, bridges, switches, and gaps that can send your block off the board. Your block can stand upright or lie flat, so knowing when to change its position is important. As the levels continue, the paths become more complex and require more planning. You may need to use special tiles or switches to open a route and reach areas that were not available at the start.

Bloxorz is mainly about planning your moves instead of rushing through a stage. A single wrong roll can put the block in a bad position or make it fall from the platform. The game also rewards players who look at the whole board before moving. Some levels can be solved in just a few moves, while others require a long sequence of careful rolls. If you enjoy puzzle games that make you stop and think, Bloxorz offers plenty of stages to work through.

How to Play

Use the arrow keys to move the block around the level. Roll it toward the goal while keeping it on the platform. When the block is standing upright, it takes up one tile. When it is lying down, it covers two tiles. Use this difference to cross paths and line up the block with the target hole.

Watch the edges before every move. Some platforms are narrow, so one extra roll can end the level. Take time to check switches and special tiles when they appear. They can change the board and give you access to another part of the stage.

The main challenge is finding the correct order of moves. If you get stuck, restart the level and try a different route. With each stage, you will get more familiar with how the block moves and how to plan around tight spaces.
