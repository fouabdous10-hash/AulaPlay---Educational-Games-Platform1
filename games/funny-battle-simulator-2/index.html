About Funny Battle Simulator 2

Funny Battle Simulator 2 is a strategy battle game where you build an army and send your units into fights against enemy forces. Instead of directly controlling one fighter, you decide which units to use and where to place them before the battle starts. Your goal is to create a formation that can defeat the opposing army. The game uses a playful style, with unusual units and entertaining battle scenes that make each match fun to watch.

Before every battle, you can study the enemy formation and choose units that fit the situation. Different fighters have different roles, such as close-range attacks, ranged support, or defense. Where you place each unit can also affect how the battle develops. Some levels may give you a limited amount of money or a set number of units, so you need to decide how to spend your resources. As you progress, enemy formations can change and may require you to experiment with new combinations.

Funny Battle Simulator 2 is simple to understand, but finding a good army setup can take several attempts. You can watch your formation in action and see which units perform well and which ones need more support. A strong group of fighters may not work if they are placed in the wrong positions. The game encourages you to test different ideas instead of using the same formation every time. If you enjoy casual strategy games with funny characters and army-building puzzles, this is a good game to try.

How to Play

Start each level by checking the enemy units and the available battlefield. Select your fighters and place them where they can perform their roles effectively. Front-line units can protect your other troops, while ranged fighters can attack from behind.

Think about the enemy formation before spending your available resources. If the opponent has many close-range units, consider adding fighters that can attack from a distance. If they have ranged attackers, use units that can reach them quickly or provide protection for your army.

Start the battle when your formation is ready and watch how the units interact. If your army loses, look at what went wrong and change the placement or unit combination. Try different setups until you find a plan that works. Continue through the stages and see how creative you can get with your army.
