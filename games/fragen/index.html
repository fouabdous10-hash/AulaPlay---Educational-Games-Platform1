About FRAGEN

FRAGEN is a browser first-person shooter where you enter competitive matches and fight against other players across different maps. The game focuses on quick gunfights, movement, and staying aware of what is happening around you. Your main goal is to defeat opponents and help your team win the match. You can move through the map, choose from available weapons, and use the surroundings to find useful positions during each round.

The maps include open spaces, buildings, paths, and corners where players can move or take cover. Different weapons can suit different situations, so choosing when to use each one is part of the challenge. You may need to fight at close range in narrow areas or aim across longer sections of the map. Matches can change quickly when several players meet in one area. Staying near teammates and checking your surroundings can help you avoid being caught by surprise.

FRAGEN is easy to start if you have played browser FPS games before, but improving your results takes practice. You need to work on aiming, movement, positioning, and knowing when to attack or retreat. Learning the maps can also help because familiar routes make it easier to predict where opponents may appear. If you enjoy online shooting games with quick rounds and competitive matches, FRAGEN gives you a straightforward FPS experience that you can play directly in your browser.

How to Play

Use the movement keys to explore the map and your mouse to aim and shoot. Keep your weapon ready when moving around corners or entering rooms. Try to use walls and other objects as cover instead of staying in open areas for too long.

Pay attention to where your teammates are and watch for enemy movement. Choose your weapon based on the distance and space around you. In close areas, quick reactions can help, while open sections may require more careful aiming.

Learn the layout of each map as you play. Remember common paths and check likely positions before moving forward. Work with your team when possible and avoid rushing into areas without checking them. With practice, you can improve your aim, movement, and positioning and become more useful during each match.
