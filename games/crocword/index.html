About Crocword

Crocword is a word puzzle game where you connect letters to create words and complete crossword-style puzzles. Your main goal is to find the hidden words from the letters shown at the bottom of the screen and use them to fill the empty spaces on the board. The game starts with simple word combinations, making it easy to understand, but later puzzles can require more thought. If you enjoy word games that let you play at your own pace, Crocword is a nice choice for a quick browser session.

Each puzzle gives you a group of letters that can be joined in different ways. Swipe or drag across the letters to form a word, then place it into the matching space on the puzzle board. Some words may be obvious, while others can take a little searching. The puzzles gradually introduce more spaces and different letter combinations as you progress. You can also discover extra words that are not part of the main puzzle, giving you another reason to test different combinations.

Crocword is more about finding patterns and building words than rushing against a clock. Take your time to look at the available letters and think about possible combinations. If you get stuck, try changing the order of the letters or look for common short words first. Completing one section can reveal more of the puzzle and give you clues for the remaining spaces. With many puzzles to work through, Crocword can be a good pick for players who enjoy casual word challenges.

How to Play

Look at the letters shown on the screen and connect them in the correct order to make a word. Drag your finger or mouse across the letters, then release when you have formed a word.

Check the crossword spaces to see how many letters are needed. Use the letters already placed on the board as clues for finding words you have not solved yet. Try different combinations when you cannot see an answer right away.

Use hints when you are stuck and continue filling the puzzle until all the required words are found. You can also try extra letter combinations to discover bonus words and increase your score.
