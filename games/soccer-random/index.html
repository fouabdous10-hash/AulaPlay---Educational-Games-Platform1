About Soccer Random

Soccer Random is a simple two-player football game built around short matches, funny movements, and unpredictable shots. Your goal is to score more goals than your opponent before the match ends. Each side controls a player who can jump, kick, and move with just one main control. The unusual physics make every kick different, so even a simple shot can lead to a surprising result. It is easy to start playing, making it a good choice for quick browser matches.

Each round takes place on a small football field with a goal on both sides. Players can jump toward the ball and try to send it into the opponent's net. The game can change parts of the match after a goal, including the field, player size, ball type, or other match conditions. These changes keep the rounds from becoming repetitive. Sometimes the ball may bounce in an unexpected direction, while another round may give you a very different setup.

The main mode is designed for two players, so you can challenge a friend on the same device. You can also play against the computer when you want a solo match. There are no long campaigns or complicated controls to learn. Instead, Soccer Random focuses on quick rounds where one good kick can change the score. Matches are short, so you can play several rounds and try different ways to control your player and reach the target.

How to Play

Press the game control to make your player jump and kick. Time your movement so the player connects with the ball at the right moment. Try to send the ball over or around the other player and toward the goal. You can also jump to block incoming shots when the ball is heading toward your net.

Do not worry if your first few kicks go in strange directions. The physics are part of the fun. Watch how the ball moves and adjust your timing on the next play. When the field or ball changes, take a moment to understand the new setup. If you are playing with a friend, take turns attacking and defending while trying to score enough goals to win the match.
