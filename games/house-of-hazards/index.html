About House of Hazards

House of Hazards is a fun multiplayer game where everyday tasks turn into a series of silly traps and surprises. You play as a small character trying to complete simple jobs around a house while other players create hazards to stop you. The main goal is to finish your tasks and reach the exit without getting caught by dangerous objects. The house is full of moving traps, flying items, and sudden obstacles that can make even a short walk difficult.

The game can be played with friends, making each round a funny competition. Players move around different rooms and look for tasks that need to be completed. At the same time, they can activate traps and cause trouble for other players. Hazards may appear from different directions, so you need to watch your surroundings while moving through the house. Each round can turn out differently because players control many of the hazards.

House of Hazards is easy to pick up, but reaching the end of a round can take some practice. The rooms have different layouts and traps that require careful movement. You may need to wait for the right moment before crossing a dangerous area or starting a task. Quick reactions are useful, but good timing can also save your character from an unexpected trap. If you enjoy short multiplayer games with funny moments, this one is worth trying.

How to Play

Use the movement controls to explore the house and complete the tasks shown during the round. Keep an eye on moving objects and avoid traps whenever possible. If you are playing against other people, pay attention to their actions because they may trigger hazards in your path. Try to move at a good time instead of rushing into every room. Complete your tasks, stay away from trouble, and make your way toward the finish.

House of Hazards is a good choice for players who enjoy simple controls, quick rounds, and playful multiplayer action. The mix of tasks and player-controlled traps keeps every match unpredictable without making the controls difficult to learn.
