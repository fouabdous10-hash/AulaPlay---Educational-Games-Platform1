About Raft Wars 2

Raft Wars 2 is a fun physics shooting game where you help the brothers continue their adventure on the water. The goal is to defeat enemies by firing from your raft and landing accurate shots. You control the angle and power of each launch, then watch the projectile travel toward its target. A good hit can knock an enemy down or push them away from their position. The cartoon style and simple controls make the game easy to pick up, while the changing targets keep each stage interesting.

In each level, enemies are placed around beaches, platforms, and other areas. Some targets are standing in open spots, while others are protected by objects. You need to study the layout before choosing where to aim. The game uses simple physics, so the path of your projectile depends on both angle and power. As you progress, the stages introduce new positions and obstacles that require more careful shots. You may also need to use objects around the level to reach targets that are not easy to hit directly.

The main challenge in Raft Wars 2 is clearing every enemy before you run out of useful shots. Try different angles when a direct attack does not work. Hitting the right spot can sometimes cause one enemy to affect another, giving you a useful chain of hits. Each completed level moves you further into the adventure and gives you another shooting puzzle to solve.

How to Play

Use your mouse to aim the launcher and control the power of each shot. Pull back to set the direction and strength, then release to fire. Watch the projectile and use the result to adjust your next shot. Aim for enemies or nearby objects that can help move the target.

Take your time when setting up important shots. A small change in angle can send the projectile to a very different spot. Try to use your shots wisely and look for chances to hit more than one target. If you enjoy simple browser games with shooting, physics, and short levels, Raft Wars 2 is worth trying.
