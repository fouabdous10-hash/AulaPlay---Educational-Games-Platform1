About Escape Road 3

Escape Road 3 is a browser driving game about escaping a police chase while racing through city streets. You take control of a car and try to stay ahead of the vehicles following you. The main goal is to keep driving, avoid crashes, and survive the chase for as long as possible. Streets can quickly become crowded, so you need to steer carefully and choose a clear route whenever you can. The game is simple to start, but the chase keeps you busy from the first turn.

As you drive through the city, you will deal with traffic, buildings, barriers, and police vehicles that try to block your path. You can change direction at intersections and use different streets to create some distance from the chase. The city layout gives you several routes, but not every road is safe. Coins and other useful items can also appear during a run, giving you extra reasons to explore the streets. The longer you remain on the road, the more difficult it becomes to avoid being stopped.

Escape Road 3 is made for quick attempts, so you can jump back into the chase after a failed run. The controls are simple, but good steering and timing are important when police cars start surrounding you. You may need to make sudden turns or change your route when traffic blocks the way. If you enjoy driving games with police chases and simple controls, Escape Road 3 gives you plenty of chances to test your reactions and try for a longer escape.

How to Play

Use the movement controls to steer your car through the city. Keep your eyes on the road and look for open spaces between traffic. When a police car gets close, turn onto another street or find a clear route that gives you room to drive.

Avoid hitting other cars, buildings, and barriers whenever possible. Crashes can slow your car and make it easier for the police to catch you. At intersections, decide where to go before making your turn. If a road looks crowded, changing direction early can help you avoid trouble.

Try to keep the chase going for as long as you can. Collect coins or other items when they are safely within your path, but do not take unnecessary risks. Each run can help you learn how the city roads work and improve your steering. Keep practicing and see how long you can stay ahead of the police.
