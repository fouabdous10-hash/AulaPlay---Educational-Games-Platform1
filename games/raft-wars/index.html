About Raft Wars

Raft Wars is a fun shooting game where you help two brothers defend their raft and fight against enemies standing on land. The main goal is to aim your shots carefully and knock out the opponents before they can stop you. You use a simple slingshot-style cannon to launch projectiles from the raft. Each shot needs the right angle and power, so taking a moment to judge the distance can make a big difference. The game has a playful cartoon look, which keeps the action light and easy to follow.

As you move through the levels, the enemies are placed in different spots and may stand behind objects or on raised platforms. You need to adjust your aim based on their position. A successful hit can push an enemy backward or knock them off their position. Some stages also give you useful objects that can change how your shots work. The challenge grows as you progress, so you need to pay attention to both power and shooting angle.

Raft Wars is mainly about completing each stage with accurate shots. You can experiment with different angles and power levels until you find a good way to clear the screen. The controls are simple, making the game easy to pick up in a browser. If you enjoy physics-based shooting games with short levels and funny characters, Raft Wars is a nice choice for a quick gaming session.

How to Play

Use your mouse to aim the slingshot and set the power of each shot. Hold and move the pointer to choose the direction, then release to fire. Watch where the projectile lands and change your aim for the next attempt. Try to hit enemies directly or use nearby objects to help your shots reach them. Some levels require careful timing because enemies can be positioned in tricky places.

Try to finish each level using as few shots as possible. Missing a target can give the enemies a chance to attack, so accurate aiming is important. Keep adjusting your angle and power when a shot does not land where you want. With simple controls, short stages, and plenty of targets to knock down, Raft Wars is a great browser game for players who enjoy casual shooting challenges.
