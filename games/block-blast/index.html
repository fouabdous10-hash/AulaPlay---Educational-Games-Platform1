About Block Blast

Block Blast is a simple puzzle game where you place blocks on a grid and try to clear as many lines as possible. Your goal is to fit the available shapes onto the board, complete full rows or columns, and remove them to make room for new pieces. There is no need to race against another player. Instead, you can take a moment to look at the board and decide where each block should go. The challenge comes from keeping enough open space for the shapes that appear next.

Each turn gives you several block shapes to place on the grid. Some are small and easy to fit, while others can take up several spaces in a row or column. Once a complete line is filled, it disappears and gives you more room. Clearing several lines through one move can help you build a high score. The board gradually becomes harder to manage because empty spaces can get separated into awkward shapes. If you run out of room for the available pieces, the game ends, so planning ahead is important.

Block Blast is easy to understand but can take some thought when the board starts filling up. You do not have to worry about complicated rules or a long tutorial. Just pick a shape, place it, and look at the next pieces before making another move. Sometimes the best choice is not the move that clears a line immediately. Keeping useful spaces open can give you more options later. If you enjoy casual puzzle games where every move matters, Block Blast is a good browser game for short sessions or longer attempts at a high score.

How to Play

Choose one of the available block shapes and place it on an open part of the grid. Try to complete full rows or columns so they disappear and free up space. Look at all the available pieces before placing one, especially when the board is getting crowded. Avoid filling the middle or corners without thinking about how future shapes may fit. If you can clear more than one line with a single placement, take the opportunity when it is safe. Keep enough open space for large shapes and continue placing blocks until no available piece can fit.
