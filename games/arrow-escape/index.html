About Arrow Escape

Arrow Escape is a puzzle game where your job is to clear a board filled with arrows. Each arrow can only move in the direction it points. Tap an arrow when its path is clear, and it slides away from the board. The goal is to remove every arrow without getting stuck or wasting all your hearts.

The first level gives you a small puzzle so you can learn how the game works. Soon, the boards become much more crowded, with many arrows blocking each other. You need to look at the paths carefully and decide which arrow should move first. Some arrows may be blocked at the start, but removing another arrow can open their path. Every level also has a timer, so you cannot spend forever thinking about one move.

You start each level with three hearts. Trying to move a blocked arrow costs one heart, so random tapping can quickly cause trouble. The game also includes useful boosters such as hints and an eraser. Larger boards can be zoomed in when the arrows become difficult to see. As you complete levels, you earn coins that can be used for extra hearts and boosters. The puzzles start simple, but later boards can become quite challenging.

How to Play

On PC, use your mouse to click an arrow and move it away. You can use the mouse wheel to zoom in or out on large boards. On mobile devices, tap an arrow to move it and use pinch gestures to zoom. Before making a move, check the arrow's full path. If another arrow is in the way, choose a different one first. Try to clear arrows that are blocking several other paths. Save your boosters for tricky situations where a single move can open the board.
