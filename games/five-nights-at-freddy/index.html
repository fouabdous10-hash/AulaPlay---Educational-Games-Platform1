About Five Nights at Freddy's

Five Nights at Freddy's is a survival horror game where you work as a night security guard at a strange family restaurant. Your main goal is to survive each night while watching the building through security cameras. The restaurant looks quiet after closing time, but the animatronic characters can move around the building when you are not watching. You have a limited amount of power, so every action needs to be considered carefully.

The game is divided into several nights, with each night lasting until morning. You can switch between different camera views to check rooms and track the animatronics. The office has doors and lights that can help you protect yourself, but using them consumes power. If you waste too much electricity, your defenses may stop working before the night ends. As the nights continue, the animatronics become more active and their movement patterns require closer attention. Learning when to check cameras and when to save power is an important part of surviving.

Five Nights at Freddy's does not rely on complicated controls. Most of the challenge comes from watching, listening, and making decisions at the right time. The limited power supply adds another problem because you cannot keep every defense active throughout the night. Each new night introduces more pressure and gives you another pattern to understand. If you enjoy spooky games that reward careful observation and patience, this is a classic browser horror experience to try.

How to Play

Use the security cameras to check different rooms around the restaurant. Pay attention to where the animatronics are and notice how their positions change between camera checks. Keep track of your power level because the building's systems use electricity.

Use the office lights and doors when you need to check or protect an entrance. Avoid using them without a reason, since unnecessary power use can leave you without enough electricity later in the night. Try to learn the movement patterns of each animatronic as you progress.

Your objective is to survive until the night ends. If you fail, think about which actions used too much power or which movement you missed. Each night gives you another chance to improve your strategy. Stay focused, manage your resources, and see if you can make it through all the nights.
