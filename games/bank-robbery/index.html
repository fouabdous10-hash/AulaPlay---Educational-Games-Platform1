About Bank Robbery

Bank Robbery is a first-person shooting game where you join a criminal crew for a risky bank heist. The main goal is to help your team take the money and survive the police attack that follows. You are not simply walking into the bank and grabbing cash. Once the robbery starts, armed police will arrive and try to stop your crew. You need to fight your way through the building and keep the assault going.

The game gives you a range of weapons to use during the robbery. You can buy weapons and then equip them before heading into action. You can switch between weapons during a mission, which helps when enemies attack from different positions. The game also lets you run, jump, crouch, and aim down your sights. These controls make it useful to move around the bank instead of standing in one spot.

Bank Robbery uses a level-based setup where each mission puts you through another part of the heist. As you progress, the police response can become more difficult to handle. Your team needs to hold its position while the robbery continues. Cash earned from the action can help you get access to useful weapons and support. The game was made by justaliendev and runs in a desktop web browser.

How to Play

Use WASD to move around the bank. Use the left mouse button to shoot and the right mouse button to aim through your weapon sights. Press Left Shift to run and Space to jump. Press C to crouch. You can switch weapons with the mouse wheel or keys 1-5. Press E when you need to take a weapon.

Keep moving when the police enter the bank and watch the areas around you. Use cover when you need a moment to aim. Choose your weapon based on the situation and help your crew survive until the robbery is complete.
