About Crazy Cattle 3D

Crazy Cattle 3D is a funny physics-based battle royale where you control an explosive sheep and compete against a large group of other sheep. The goal is simple: stay on the map and be the last sheep standing. There are no guns, vehicles, or large inventories to manage. Instead, your main weapon is movement. You need to build momentum, ram other sheep, and use the terrain to send them flying off the map.

The game takes place across three different environments: Ireland, Iceland, and New Zealand. Ireland has rolling green hills that are useful for building speed before a charge. Iceland has volcanic areas and rough ground that can make movement risky. New Zealand has steep mountains and narrow areas where one bad move can end your run. Each map changes how you approach fights, so learning the terrain is just as important as controlling your sheep.

Crazy Cattle 3D does not use traditional upgrades or weapons. Your progress depends on how well you control your sheep and understand its movement. Other sheep can attack at any time, so you need to watch their direction and avoid getting pushed toward an edge. Some matches can quickly turn into a messy group fight, while other moments give you a chance to pick your target carefully. The simple idea makes every round unpredictable, especially when several sheep collide at once.

How to Play

Use WASD or the Arrow Keys to move your sheep. Use the Mouse to control your direction. Hold Spacebar to charge into other sheep and create a strong collision. Build momentum by moving across slopes, then use that speed when you attack.

Try to stay away from map edges unless you are pushing another sheep toward them. Watch the terrain and look for good slopes before making a charge. You do not need to attack everyone right away. Sometimes staying in a safe area and letting other sheep knock each other out is a good plan. Once only a few sheep remain, choose your attacks carefully and try to be the final one standing.
