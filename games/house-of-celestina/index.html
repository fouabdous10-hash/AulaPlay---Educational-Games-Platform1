About House of Celestina

House of Celestina is a horror escape game where you enter a mysterious house and try to find a way out while dealing with strange events. The building has dark rooms, locked doors, hidden objects, and clues that can help you understand what to do next. Your main goal is to explore the house, collect useful items, solve puzzles, and reach the exit without getting caught. The house can be confusing, so remembering where you have been is an important part of the game.

While exploring, search rooms carefully for keys, tools, and other objects. Some items may be hidden inside drawers, on shelves, or in places that are easy to overlook. You may need one object to open a door and another to solve a puzzle in the next room. The house also has different paths to explore, so it is useful to check areas before moving too far ahead. Strange sounds and unexpected events can happen as you investigate, making each trip through the house a little uncertain.

House of Celestina combines exploration, puzzle solving, hiding, and horror in a simple browser adventure. You need to think about where to go while keeping an eye on your surroundings. If you get caught or miss an important item, you can use what you learned during another attempt. The more you learn about the house, the easier it becomes to plan your route. If you enjoy creepy escape games with hidden objects, puzzles, and mysterious locations, House of Celestina is worth trying.

How to Play

Explore the house and check each room for objects that can help you escape. Search drawers, shelves, tables, cabinets, and other areas carefully. Pick up useful items and look for doors or puzzles where they may be needed. Remember the locations of important objects because you may need to return to them later.

Listen for sounds while moving through the house and be ready to change your route when something feels unsafe. Look for places where you can hide if necessary. If you get stuck, return to earlier rooms and search again for clues or items you may have missed. Solve the puzzles, open new areas, and keep working toward the exit.
